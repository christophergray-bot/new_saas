Family Quest Center
Sprint 3.0-G.1A — Mission Builder Family Isolation

This is a code-only patch. It does not replace chorce.db or delete family data.

FIXED
- New Mission Builder schedules now store the active family_id.
- Mission Builder lists only schedules owned by the signed-in family.
- Schedule view, edit, archive, and restore actions require matching family ownership.
- Mission generation processes only schedules owned by the active family.
- Selected children and quests are validated as belonging to the active family.
- Existing schedules are repaired at startup using matching child and quest ownership.
- Ambiguous cross-family schedules are disabled instead of being exposed.
- Archived quest schedules are family-scoped.
- Clean installations include the Mission Builder family ownership column and index.

INSTALLATION
1. Stop Family Quest Center.
2. Extract this ZIP.
3. Copy the included files and folders into the existing application folder.
4. Allow Windows to replace matching files.
5. Do not copy, replace, or delete chorce.db.
6. Restart Family Quest Center. The schedule repair runs automatically.

IMPORTANT
Existing schedules whose child and quest belong to different families are disabled.
Recreate those schedules under the correct family if needed.
