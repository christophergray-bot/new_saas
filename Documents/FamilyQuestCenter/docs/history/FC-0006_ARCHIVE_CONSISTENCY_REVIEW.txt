FC-0006 — GLOBAL ARCHIVE MANAGEMENT STANDARD

Completed review scope:
- Children
- Quests
- Adventures
- Family Challenges
- Rewards and Reward Categories
- Achievements
- Quest Schedules
- Adventure Schedules

Standard applied:
1. New manageable content is active by default.
2. Archive/Restore terminology is used consistently instead of delete, retire, disable, pause, or deactivate.
3. Archived records remain visible to parents through All/Archived filters.
4. Archived records are removed from active child gameplay and new assignment selections.
5. Completed assignments, earned achievements, reward history, and audit history are preserved.
6. Active records sort before archived records where supported.
7. Archive actions use clear status badges and confirmations where history or gameplay may be affected.

Engineering findings addressed:
- Added search and Active/Archived filters to Family Members and Family Challenges.
- Standardized child profile, reward, reward category, and quest schedule actions to Archive/Restore.
- Standardized archived status labels across quests, adventures, challenges, rewards, achievements, schedules, and children.
- Added explicit success messages for adventure, challenge, reward, reward category, child, and schedule archive/restore actions.

No destructive database migration is required.
