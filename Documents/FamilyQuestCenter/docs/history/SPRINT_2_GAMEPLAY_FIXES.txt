Family Quest Center v1.0.0 — Sprint 2 Gameplay Fixes

Resolved in this build:
- FC-0001: Daily missions, adventure missions, and family challenges now use distinct badges.

- FC-0008: Parents can reorder adventure quests with Move Up/Move Down controls. Children see completed, current, waiting, and locked progression states. Completing a step immediately unlocks the next; approval remains responsible for rewards.

Database migration: None required.
