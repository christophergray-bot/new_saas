Family Quest Center RC-0014 — Adventure Lifecycle Redesign

Implemented:
- Children complete Chapters directly without parent approval.
- Completing a Chapter awards its Chapter XP immediately.
- The next Chapter unlocks immediately.
- Completing the final Chapter submits the entire Adventure for Quest Master celebration.
- The Approval Center now shows Adventure Celebration items instead of Chapter approvals.
- Celebrating an Adventure marks it complete and awards the Adventure bonus XP.
- Returning an Adventure reopens its final Chapter.
- Child dashboards show Adventures awaiting celebration and completed Adventures.

Database upgrade:
The application automatically adds pending_approval, submitted_at, and parent_message fields to adventure_instances_v2. Existing family data is preserved.

Current reward behavior:
This build awards the existing Chapter XP and Adventure bonus XP. Adventure-specific badges, trophies, Stars, and reward objects can be connected later once those reward fields are added to the Adventure model.
