RC1.9.1 - Parent and Family Settings Reorganization

Parent Management now contains parent-specific tools: notifications, parent activity, parent account details, profile security, and Parent Two invitations.

My Profile now contains account details, Change Password, and Change Parent PIN.

Family Settings now contains Family Identity, Quest Defaults, Operations, Experience & Progression, Session, and the future Sessions and Trusted Devices area.

Legacy Parent Settings and Parent Security URLs redirect to the new destinations.
