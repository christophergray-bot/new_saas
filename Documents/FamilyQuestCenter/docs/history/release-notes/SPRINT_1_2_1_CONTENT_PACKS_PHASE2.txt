Family Quest Center v1.2.1 — Content Packs Phase 2

Implemented:
- Archive an installed Content Pack as a unit.
- Restore an archived Content Pack as a unit.
- Preserve completed history and family progress.
- Preserve items that were already archived individually.
- Restore only the items that were archived by the pack action.
- Maintain family-specific pack status and source tracking.
- Upgrade existing content_pack_items tables with pack_archived tracking.

Behavior:
- Archive Pack archives currently active pack quests, adventures, challenges, and rewards.
- An item already archived individually is left untouched.
- Restore Pack reactivates only items archived by Archive Pack.
- Individual archive/restore controls remain available in each library.
- No user-created family content is deleted.
