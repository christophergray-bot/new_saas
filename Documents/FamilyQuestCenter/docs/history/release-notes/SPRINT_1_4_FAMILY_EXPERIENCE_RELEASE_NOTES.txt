Family Quest Center v1.4.0 — Family Experience

Parent: Family Setup Adventure
- New registrations proceed from Parent PIN creation to a resumable setup checklist.
- Checklist detects the first child, first assigned mission, first assigned adventure, and active rewards.
- Parent approval guidance is acknowledged directly in the checklist.
- Final action locks the Parent Area and opens a child-safe completion screen.
- Enter Family Quest Center opens the Family Dashboard in locked mode.
- Incomplete setups return to the checklist after normal sign-in/unlock.

Child: Explorer Training
- The first time each child profile is opened, a seven-step tutorial appears.
- It explains the Quest Board, Adventures, Chapters, completion, approvals, Stars, Rewards, and Achievements.
- Training is stored per child and does not repeat after completion.

Database
- Adds family_onboarding and child_training tables automatically at startup.
- No existing family data is deleted or replaced.
