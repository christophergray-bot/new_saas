RC1.9.2 Parent Management Live Pages

- Notifications and Parent Activity cards now open their working pages instead of showing Coming Soon.
- Parent Activity is scoped to the authenticated family.
- Audit records now store family_id for future parent activity entries.
- Existing legacy audit rows without family_id are intentionally excluded to prevent cross-family exposure.

4. Sign into a second family and confirm the first family activity is not visible.
