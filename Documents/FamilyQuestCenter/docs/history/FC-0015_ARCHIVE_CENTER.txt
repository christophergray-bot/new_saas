FC-0015 — ARCHIVE CENTER
Family Quest Center v1.0.0 RC1

Implemented:
- Added a dedicated Parent Archive Center.
- Added archive cards for Family Members, Quests, Adventures, Challenges, Rewards, Achievements, and Schedules.
- Added searchable category pages containing only archived records.
- Added restore actions for every supported archived record type.
- Combined quest and adventure schedules in the Schedules archive.
- Included archived reward categories in the Rewards archive.
- Simplified primary management pages so they display active records only.
- Added Archive Center access to the Parent Command Center.
- Preserved historical assignments, completions, achievement unlocks, reward requests, and activity records.

Database migration: Not required. Existing active flags are reused.
