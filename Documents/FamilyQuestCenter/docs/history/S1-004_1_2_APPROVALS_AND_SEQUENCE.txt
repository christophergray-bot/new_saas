S1-004.1 / S1-004.2 — Unified Approvals and Sequential Journeys

Included:
- Version 2 Journeys are submitted to the parent Approval Center before XP is awarded.
- Approval Center combines Missions, legacy Adventure steps, and Version 2 Journeys.
- Approval filters: All, Missions, Journeys.
- Approving a Journey awards Journey XP and unlocks only the next Journey.
- Approving the final Journey completes the Adventure and awards bonus XP.
- Returning a Journey makes it available to the child again.
- Adventure Builder now supports explicit Journey ordering with Move Up and Move Down controls.
- Existing Adventures can be edited when they have no active assignments.
- Children cannot skip locked Journeys.
- A submitted Journey remains visible as Waiting for Approval.

Database compatibility:
- Existing journey_instances tables receive pending_approval and submitted_at columns automatically.
- No manual database migration is required.
