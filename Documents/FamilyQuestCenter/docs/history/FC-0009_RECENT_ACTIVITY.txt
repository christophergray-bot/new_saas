FC-0009 — Improved Recent Activity Widget

Implemented:
- Clear child name, action, and activity title hierarchy.
- Activity-type badges for Daily Mission, Adventure Mission, Adventure, Reward, Progress, and achievement category.
- Reward summaries for Stars and XP when available.
- Achievement-unlocked events.
- Adventure-started and adventure-completed events.
- Reward requested, approved, denied, and redeemed wording.
- Responsive activity card layout for smaller screens.

No database migration is required. Activity is assembled from existing tables.
