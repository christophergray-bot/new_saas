TENANT ISOLATION TEST CHECKLIST

Prepare two separate family accounts: Family A and Family B.

1. In Family A, create a child, mission, adventure, challenge, reward request,
   and at least one completed item awaiting approval.
2. Sign out completely.
3. Sign in as Family B.
4. Confirm Family B shows zero Family A items in:
   - Pending Approvals
   - Active Missions
   - Active Adventures
   - Active Challenges
   - Recent Activity
   - Weekly Stats
   - Reward Requests
5. Create one item of each type in Family B.
6. Sign back into Family A and confirm Family B items are not visible.
7. Try direct URLs using IDs copied from the other family. The app should
   return Not Found or deny access.
8. Repeat with Parent Two where available.
9. Confirm creating, editing, archiving, approving, returning, and completing
   records only affects the signed-in family.

Release gate
Do not begin PostgreSQL conversion until both-family testing passes with no
cross-family records displayed or modified.
