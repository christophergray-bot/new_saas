# RC1.9.1 Parent and Family Settings Reorganization

## Parent Management
- Confirm Security card is removed.
- Confirm Notifications and Parent Activity open normally.
- Confirm Edit My Profile opens My Profile.
- Confirm My Profile shows Account and Security sections.
- Change the account password and verify sign-in is required afterward.
- Change the Parent PIN and verify the new PIN unlocks parent tools.
- Confirm Parent Two invitation/removal behavior is unchanged.

## Family Settings
- Confirm Family Identity saves application/family name and regional preferences.
- Confirm Quest Defaults save and prefill a new quest.
- Confirm Operations saves daily reset and streak grace days.
- Confirm Experience & Progression saves XP awards.
- Confirm Session timeout saves.
- Confirm Lock Parent Area signs out/locks correctly.
- Confirm Sessions and Trusted Devices appears as a future placeholder.

## Legacy URLs
- `/parent/settings` redirects to Family Settings.
- `/parent/management/security` redirects to My Profile Security.
