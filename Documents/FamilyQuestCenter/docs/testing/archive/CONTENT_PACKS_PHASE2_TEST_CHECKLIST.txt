Content Packs Phase 2 Manual Test Checklist

1. Start with a family that has not installed Explorer Starter Pack.
2. Install Explorer Starter Pack.
3. Confirm the pack status is Active.
4. Confirm pack quests, adventures, challenges, and rewards appear in their libraries.
5. Individually archive one pack quest from Quest Library.
6. Return to Content Packs and archive Explorer Starter Pack.
7. Confirm remaining active pack items are archived.
8. Restore Explorer Starter Pack.
9. Confirm items archived by the pack are active again.
10. Confirm the quest archived individually remains archived.
11. Confirm completed history and child progress remain available.
12. Sign into a different family and confirm its pack status/content is independent.
