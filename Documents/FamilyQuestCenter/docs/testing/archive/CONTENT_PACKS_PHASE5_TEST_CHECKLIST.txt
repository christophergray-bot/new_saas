Content Packs Phase 5 — Manual Test Checklist

1. Start with a clean database.
2. Open Create Family Account.
3. Confirm Explorer Starter Pack is selected by default.
4. Register Family A with Explorer Starter Pack.
5. Create the Parent PIN and open Content Packs.
6. Confirm Explorer Starter Pack is installed and active.
7. Confirm starter quests, adventures, challenges, rewards, pack achievements, avatars, frames, and theme are available.
8. Sign out.
9. Register Family B and choose Empty Family.
10. Create the Parent PIN.
11. Confirm Explorer Starter Pack is not installed.
12. Confirm Family B sees only universal core achievements and no starter quests/adventures/challenges/rewards.
13. Confirm Family A still sees its installed content.
14. Install Explorer Starter Pack manually for Family B from Content Packs and confirm successful installation.
