Adventure-First System Manual Test

1. Start with a clean database and create a family and two children.
2. Open Adventure Library. Confirm there is no separate Journey Library card.
3. Create Adventure: Become a Student Cook.
4. Add at least three Chapters, reorder them, and save.
5. Confirm no child was assigned during creation.
6. From Adventure Library, select both children and assign the Adventure.
7. Open each child profile. Confirm the Adventure name appears before the current Chapter.
8. Confirm each child shows Chapter 1 of 3 independently.
9. Complete/approve Chapter 1 for only one child. Confirm only that child advances.
10. Confirm the other child remains on Chapter 1.
11. Try editing an Adventure with an active assignment; confirm the system blocks Chapter changes.
12. Complete all Chapters and confirm Adventure completion bonus is awarded.
13. Verify a second family cannot see or assign the first family's Adventure.
