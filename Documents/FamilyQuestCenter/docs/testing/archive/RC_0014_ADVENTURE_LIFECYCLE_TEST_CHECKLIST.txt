RC-0014 Adventure Lifecycle Test Checklist

1. Create an Adventure with at least 3 Chapters.
2. Assign it to a child.
3. Open the child profile. Confirm only Chapter 1 is available.
4. Click Complete Chapter.
5. Confirm Chapter 1 completes immediately and Chapter 2 unlocks without parent approval.
6. Repeat for Chapter 2. Confirm Chapter 3 unlocks immediately.
7. Complete the final Chapter.
8. Confirm the child sees Awaiting Celebration and no additional Chapter.
9. Open Parent Approval Center.
10. Confirm one Adventure Celebration item appears, not individual Chapter approvals.
11. Click Celebrate Adventure.
12. Confirm the Adventure becomes completed and the Adventure bonus XP is awarded.
13. Confirm the child sees the Adventure as completed.
14. Repeat with two different children and verify independent progress.
15. Verify normal Mission approvals still require parent approval.
