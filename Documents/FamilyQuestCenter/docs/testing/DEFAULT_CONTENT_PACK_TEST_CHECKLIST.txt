FAMILY QUEST CENTER — RC1.6.6 DEFAULT CONTENT PACK TEST CHECKLIST

Use a fresh database or register a brand-new family that has never installed the starter pack.

1. Register a family with Family Quest Starter Pack selected.
2. Confirm exactly one starter Quest is added: Make Your Bed.
3. Confirm the Quest is active, awards 10 Stars, and requires parent approval.
4. Confirm exactly one starter Reward is added: Choose a Treat (25 Stars).
5. Confirm exactly one starter Adventure is added: Outdoor Adventure.
6. Confirm Outdoor Adventure contains exactly three ordered Chapters:
   1 — Find Something Cool
   2 — Count the Trees
   3 — Nature Explorer
7. Confirm no starter Challenges or pack-specific Achievements are installed.
8. Confirm built-in Achievement Engine v2 goals remain available.
9. Confirm Character choices contain Boy Adventurer and Girl Adventurer.
10. Confirm only Classic Gold frame and Classic Quest theme are available by default.
11. Confirm the parent can edit/archive the example Quest, Reward, and Adventure.
12. Restart the application and confirm no duplicate starter records are created.
13. Attempt to install the starter pack again and confirm duplicate installation is prevented.
14. Register an Empty Workspace family and confirm no example Quest, Reward, or Adventure is created, while built-in cosmetics and achievements remain available.
